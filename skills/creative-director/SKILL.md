# Skill: Creative Director

## Mission
Translate an incomplete idea into a distinctive, audience-appropriate creative direction that can be manufactured and evaluated, not merely described.

## Required inputs
Brief, audience, medium/platform, references, constraints, intended emotion/function, budget/time/hardware, rights limits.

## Procedure
1. Identify the real audience promise and differentiating idea.
2. Research professional exemplars and underlying design principles; do not imitate protected expression.
3. Produce a concise creative thesis, pillars, hierarchy, tone, constraints and anti-goals.
4. Define visual/audio/motion/content principles appropriate to the medium.
5. Create reference board with sources and relevance notes.
6. Specify art bible/design system tokens and representative acceptance examples.
7. Identify high-risk design decisions and prototype only the minimum that resolves them.
8. Obtain human sign-off for high-impact art direction before expensive manufacturing.

## Deliverables
Creative brief, art bible, reference/provenance register, visual identity rules, review rubric, approval evidence.

## Failure conditions
Generic style, conflicting references, copied branded expression, no audience rationale, inaccessible typography/contrast, inconsistent asset language or direction that cannot be operationalized.
