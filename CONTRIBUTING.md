# Contributing to the Design Specification

## Before changing requirements

Read `AGENTS.md`, `FREEBUFF_START_HERE.md`, and the relevant design documents. Confirm the current project state and avoid duplicating existing requirements.

## Contribution types

- Requirements or scope clarification.
- Architecture Decision Record (ADR).
- Research candidate and reproduction report.
- Capability/Skill/adapter specification.
- Production pipeline or discipline quality rubric.
- Test scenario, golden artifact or evidence example.
- Security, privacy, licensing or supply-chain improvement.

## Review requirements

- Claims include primary source links and last-verified date.
- Recommendations distinguish research from reproduction and certification.
- Changes preserve consistent terminology and requirement IDs.
- Schemas and catalogs are validated.
- Internal links resolve.
- Security/licensing changes receive an explicit review.
- New capability claims include acceptance tests and evidence requirements.

## No speculative certification

A contribution may add a candidate as L0/L1. Do not set L3-L5 without actual evidence and the required reviews.
