# Success Metrics and Competitive Targets

## Non-negotiable gates

- 100% mandatory acceptance criteria pass for every certified capability and release in its declared support matrix.
- Zero unresolved P0 defects.
- No silent P1 waiver; every exception has approval, rationale and expiration.
- 100% of released artifacts have a provenance/license status and file integrity record.
- 100% of advertised capabilities have a maturity level and last-verified date.

## Quality and autonomy metrics

Measure baseline first; then set improvement targets. Candidate metrics:

- First-pass acceptance rate by discipline.
- Mean repair cycles before acceptance.
- Defects per artifact and severity.
- Human intervention minutes per accepted deliverable.
- Fraction of intent requirements with evidence coverage.
- Successful task completion rate across benchmark suite.
- Recovery success after timeouts/crashes.
- Cross-application conversion fidelity.
- On-device runtime reliability and resource consumption.
- User acceptance and expert-review agreement.

## Competitive superiority test

To claim the studio surpasses a reference in a dimension, run repeated matched trials under documented conditions. Compare final accepted outputs, reliability, human correction, elapsed time, compute cost, and support breadth. Publish the test conditions and failure cases. No claim of superiority based only on architecture diagrams, feature counts, or a few showcase renders.
