# End-to-End Vertical Slice — First Commercial-Quality Proof

## Why this is the first flagship deliverable

A complete but small artifact-to-game workflow tests the most difficult seams simultaneously: intent interpretation, art direction, generation, native editing, interchange, engine import, runtime behavior, objective QA, visual review, packaging and evidence. A wide tool catalog without this proof is not a production moat.

## Scope contract

Produce one original stylized or realistic micro-experience selected through a brief. Required outputs:
- approved creative brief and art bible;
- one hero character/prop with editable native source, materials and texture maps;
- one coherent environment/scene with lighting and set dressing;
- one purposeful animation or state transition;
- one audio identity with at least two implemented cues;
- one functional interaction and UI element;
- a playable target-engine project plus clean packaged build;
- evidence bundle and asset provenance/license ledger.

No primitive placeholder, stock template or one-shot generation output may be substituted for a required finished asset.

## Gate sequence

1. Intent and acceptance matrix approved.
2. Reference/license research complete.
3. Art direction approved.
4. Source assets manufactured and structurally reviewed.
5. Exports round-trip through the target engine.
6. Gameplay/interaction test passes.
7. Visual/audio craft review passes with defect log closed.
8. Performance and platform tests pass.
9. Clean-room build and launch pass.
10. Evidence and provenance package passes completeness validation.

## Quality rubric dimensions

Use the discipline-specific rubric from `docs/00-foundation/VISUAL_MOAT.md` and `docs/06-quality/VISUAL_AUDIO_REVIEW.md`. Score only with calibrated anchors and examples. An aggregate score cannot hide a P0 defect. Every dimension must have a written rationale and linked evidence. The final subjective review must be human-approved until automated review performance has been validated against a sufficiently representative labeled set.
