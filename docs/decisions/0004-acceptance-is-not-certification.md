# ADR-0004: Job Acceptance Is Not Capability/Release Certification

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-04

## Decision

A job may pass its own declared deterministic and review criteria while the adapter/model/platform matrix remains uncertified. Certification requires complete capability-specific tests, exact supported versions, security/license approval and retained evidence. A successful process exit or hash integrity check cannot represent commercial quality.
