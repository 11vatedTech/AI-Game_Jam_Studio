# Writing, Narrative, Publishing, and Brand

## Scope

Research and briefs; worldbuilding; game narrative; branching dialogue; interactive fiction; screenplay; fiction/nonfiction; technical documentation; marketing copy; product naming; brand strategy; visual identity; editorial review; localization; publishing and print/digital distribution.

## Candidate systems

Ink, Yarn Spinner, Twine, Ren'Py, standard editorial tools and content systems. Brand design should be integrated with 2D/vector and UI design tools, not reduced to a generated logo image. Evaluate grammar/style tooling and localization systems by language and target audience.

## Craft requirements

- Voice, tone, audience, genre conventions, point of view, structure, pacing, clarity, factual accuracy, character motivation, continuity and thematic coherence.
- Branching narrative must have state models, reachable paths, dead-end detection, variable coverage, localization-safe text, and testable outcomes.
- Brand systems require strategic positioning, naming checks, visual identity, typography/color rules, usage rules, accessibility and asset package.
- Technical documentation must be accurate, versioned, navigable, example-tested and consistent with the real product.

## Review layers

Research/fact check → developmental edit → structure/continuity → line edit → proofread → localization/context review → layout/accessibility → final export. Preserve sources and distinguish facts, interpretation, and creative fiction.
