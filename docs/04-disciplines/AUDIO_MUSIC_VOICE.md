# Audio, Music, Voice, and Sound Design

## Scope

Concept and sound palette, SFX, foley, ambience, UI audio, dialogue, voice recording/generation, cleanup, music composition, adaptive music, synthesis, mixing, mastering, spatial audio, interactive event routing, and delivery.

## Candidate ecosystem

Audacity, Ardour, SuperCollider, LMMS, MuseScore, REAPER (optional commercial), engine-native audio, Wwise/FMOD (optional middleware), and local model pipelines for speech/audio where permitted. Review samples, soundfonts, plugins and training data licenses separately from application licenses.

## Audio deliverable contract

- Source session/project, stems, consolidated assets and final exports as required.
- Sample rate, bit depth, channel count/layout, codec, loudness target, peak/true peak, loop points, markers, naming and metadata.
- Clean edits, no unintended clicks/clipping/distortion, appropriate noise floor and phase behavior.
- Dialogue intelligibility and consistent voice identity; generated voices require rights/consent review and disclosure where appropriate.
- Music supports narrative/gameplay and has coherent structure, transitions and adaptive layers when requested.
- Engine event routing, attenuation, spatialization, ducking, priorities and concurrency are tested in context.

## Review

Listen on representative headphones/speakers and at realistic levels. Review loudness and meters as well as perception. A waveform image is not sufficient. Preserve session/patch/source and plugin versions for repeatability.
