# VFX, Simulation, Procedural Worlds, and Compositing

## Scope

Particles, fluids, smoke, fire, destruction, cloth, rigid/soft-body dynamics, crowds, terrain, procedural placement, foliage, weather, lighting effects, motion graphics, compositing, tracking, rotoscoping, keying, camera projection, and simulation/scientific visualization.

## Candidate tools

Blender simulations and compositing, Houdini (optional professional), Unreal Niagara, Unity VFX Graph, Natron, DaVinci Resolve Fusion, Nuke (optional), engine-native particle systems, and scientific stacks where relevant.

## Quality requirements

- Effects support the visual story and remain readable; no uncontrolled noise or clutter.
- Scale, timing, physical behavior, interaction, collision, lighting and camera response are plausible or intentionally stylized.
- Simulations are deterministic or have captured seeds/cache/version where feasible.
- Caches and source setups are preserved when the workflow requires editability.
- GPU/CPU cost, overdraw, memory and runtime budgets are measured.
- Composite edges, grain, motion blur, depth integration, color and lens behavior are consistent with source plates/scene.
- Engine effects survive target resolution, camera movement, variable frame rate and gameplay state.
